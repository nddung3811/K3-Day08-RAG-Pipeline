Nguyễn Thị Hiền:  Cultural management of the practice of  Viet’s b elief in the Mother 
Goddesses of Three Realms from inter-ministerial, inter-sectoral, inter-level perspectives 
“The practice of Viet’s worship of Mother Goddesses of Three Realms” is a form of combination of both 
religion and beliefs and a heritage inscribed on the National List and the List of Intangible Cultural Heritage 
of Humanity. The cultural management of this heritage is inter -ministerial, inter -sectoral, inter -level in 
scope. The decentralization of management aims to clarify the respon sibilities of state management 
agencies for religion and heritage. The paper discovered the overlap and complexity in the cultural 
management of the practice of Viet’s worship of the Mother Goddesses of Three Realms. 
Keywords: Cultural management, The practice of Viet’s worship of the Mother Goddesses of Three 
Realms, intangible cultural heritage, Law on Cultural Heritage, Law on Religion and Beliefs.
